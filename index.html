Restores golden moments with targeted sexual wellness support.

  • L-Arginine — 250 mg
  • Fenugreek Seed Extract (Testofen®) — 150 mg
  • Tribulus Terrestris — 125 mg
  • Pine Bark Extract — 50 mg
  • Trans-Resveratrol — 50 mg
  • Saffron — 30 mg
  • Mucuna Pruriens — 5 mg
  • Zinc — 5 mg
  • BioPerine — 2.5 mg

Key benefits

  • Restores golden moments
  • Improves sexual response cycle
  • Improves sexual performance
  • Improves satisfaction
  • Shows neurogenic response

Female Sexual Dysfunction: Low libido, low arousal, less orgasm, pain during intercourse.

Male Sexual Dysfunction: Low libido, erectile dysfunction, psychogenic erectile dysfunction (ED).

02Female Fertility SupportOVAQ-FOptimizes Oocyte & Embryo Quality+

OVAQ-F Tablets

A fertility-focused formulation designed to support oocyte quality and cellular resilience.

Pack size: 10 x 1 x 10 Tablets

Composition per film coated tablet

  • Nicotinamide Mononucleotide (NMN) — 250 mg
  • Ubidecarenone (Co-Enzyme Q10) — 100 mg
  • Astaxanthin — 10 mg
  • Melatonin — 3 mg

Key benefits

  • Provides dual activation of SIRT1 & NRF2
  • Optimizes egg quality
  • Protects cellular integrity
  • Supports cellular longevity

Ideal for

Poor oocyte quality, diminished ovarian reserve, advanced maternal age (≥30–35 years), repeated miscarriage (RM), IVF/IUI failures, hyperthyroidism, obesity, diabetes and PCOS.

04Iron SupplementEROFERWay to Faster Hb Rise+

EROFER Tablets

Heme iron support with active folate, vitamin B12, and vitamin C to help support red blood cell development.

Pack size: 10 x 1 x 10 Tablets

Composition per film coated tablet

  • Heme Iron Polypeptide — 12 mg
  • L-Methyl Folate — 40 mcg
  • Vitamin B12 — 2.2 mcg
  • Zinc — 15 mg
  • Vitamin C — 80 mg

Key features

  • Absorbed through a specialized heme-receptor pathway
  • L-Methylfolate is a biologically active form of folate
  • 100% bioavailability even in MTHFR mutation patients
  • Vitamin B12 + Vitamin C + Zinc enhance absorption
  • Supports erythrocyte maturation
  • Promotes healthy fetal growth
  • Supports immune function

Indications

Iron deficiency anemia, post-partum recovery, pregnancy, lactation, pre-conception priming, dysfunctional uterine bleeding (DUB) and menorrhagia.
